## What the app stores

The app stores your habits, completed days, reminders and settings **only on your device**. We have no servers, and the app has no permission to access the internet.

## Backups

- **Android backup:** If you turn on Android backup on your device, Android may include DailyDojo's data in the backup to your own Google account. Google handles this backup under its own privacy policy. We cannot access it.
- **Export files:** When you choose Export, the app creates a file with your habits and lets you save or share it. The file goes only where you send it.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to show the reminders you set.
- **Run at startup:** to schedule your reminders again after the device restarts.

## Purchases

DailyDojo Pro is not on sale yet. If paid features become available, Google Play will process payments, and we will update this policy before then.

## Children

DailyDojo is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. This deletes all data the app has stored on your device. Copies in your Android backups or export files are under your control.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
